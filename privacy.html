Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

How tools process your information

Processing depends on the tool. Many tools process text and files locally in your browser, so the input is not uploaded to a ToolNest application server. Some tools load code or models from third-party content delivery networks, and certain features may contact external services to complete a task.

For example, the PDF translation feature sends extracted document text to the MyMemory translation service. Do not use that feature with confidential or sensitive documents. Read the instructions shown beside a tool before using it, and avoid entering information you would not want a third party to receive.

Hosting and technical information

ToolNest is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information such as request logs to operate and secure hosting. Third-party libraries and services are governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

Analytics, cookies and advertising

ToolNest does not currently intentionally run its own analytics or advertising scripts. If analytics, advertising, affiliate links, or other monetization services are introduced, this policy must be updated to explain the relevant data collection, cookies, and third-party providers before those services are enabled.

Children and sensitive information

The tools are general-purpose utilities and are not designed to collect sensitive personal information. Do not submit confidential, financial, medical, identity, or other sensitive files unless you have assessed the specific tool and any external services it uses.

Policy updates

This policy may change as the website and its features evolve. Check this page periodically. This information is a general explanation and is not legal advice.

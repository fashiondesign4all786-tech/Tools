Video to MP3 Converter

Extract an MP3 audio track from a video file. Your selected file is processed in your browser; it is not uploaded to a ToolNest server.

The conversion engine is downloaded from a public CDN on first use and may be large. Keep this page open while it converts. Larger files may be slow or exceed your browser's available memory.

How it works

Choose a video file, start conversion, and download the resulting MP3. Only convert media you own or are authorized to use. Processing depends on browser compatibility and available memory.

If conversion fails, try a smaller MP4 file or a modern desktop browser. This free browser-based workflow is provided as-is; not every codec/container combination is supported.

About ToolNest

ToolNest is a collection of lightweight utilities designed to help with common digital tasks. This first version runs its core calculations and text processing locally in your browser. Features that require a server or external service are not included.

Privacy by design

Most tools on this page process your text, images, and files locally in your browser. This page does not upload those inputs to a ToolNest server. If you later add analytics, advertising, external fonts, or third-party services, update this notice and your privacy disclosures accordingly.

Important: avoid using this demo for highly sensitive files until you have reviewed and tested the code and hosting setup yourself.
